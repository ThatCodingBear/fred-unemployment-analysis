# Which states showed the most consistent year-over-year improvement from 2023–2025?

![Results](Results.png)

## Conclusion
Only one state out of 51 showed a strict, uninterrupted year-over-year improvement across all three years. This shows that
the post-pandemic "recovery" wasn't a steady trend for most states. This insight might be missed if you only ever look at a single before/after comparison instead of year to year.
